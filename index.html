★ NUS BMF5324 · Featured Research · 2025/2026
The Influence of Financial Stress & Industrial Activity on the Gold–Silver Ratio (2016–2025)

Investigated macroeconomic drivers of the GSR using OLS, ARDL, and VECM models on 2,449 daily observations spanning Brexit, COVID-19, and the 2022–23 inflation cycle. The VIX Index and Global PMI together explain ~67% of GSR variation, confirming gold's safe-haven role and silver's pro-cyclical industrial demand channel.
